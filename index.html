Built for personal use

Stillwater is a privately operated app for its owner's accounts. It currently runs locally on the owner's Windows computer and is not open for public registration.

Investment, loan-detail, and recurring-payment views are planned. Availability depends on the connected institution and supported data.

Why account data is used

Stillwater uses Plaid to connect accounts with the owner's authorization. Account information, balances, and transaction history support spending summaries and budgets. Retrieved financial data is stored locally on the owner's computer.

This public page provides app information and a support contact. It does not provide access to the app or collect bank credentials. It uses no analytics scripts or contact forms; the website host may process routine connection information.
