Privacy notice

Effective September 11, 2026

Stillwater is a private personal-finance application operated by its owner for the owner's own accounts. It is not open for public registration. This notice covers the local application and this public information website.

Information used by the app

With the account owner's authorization, Stillwater uses Plaid to obtain supported account names, institution names, account identifiers and partial account masks, balances, and transaction history. Transaction information can include dates, descriptions, merchants, amounts, categories, currencies, and pending status. The owner may also import CSV transaction records and enter account labels, categorization rules, budgets, and savings goals.

Stillwater stores the authorization tokens and developer credentials needed to communicate with Plaid. It does not request bank login passwords or full account or routing numbers in its own forms. Bank authentication occurs through Plaid Link and, where applicable, the financial institution's authorization flow.

Purpose and storage

Information is used to display account activity, categorize spending, prepare budgets, track goals, and maintain account connections. Retrieved financial records are stored on the owner's Windows computer. The public website does not host the finance application or its financial database.

The app does not sell financial information, use it for advertising, or send it to an AI service. Development and support tools operated by the owner may have access to local project files; those tools should use synthetic data and avoid copying financial records or credentials into conversations.

Plaid and other providers

Plaid processes information when the owner connects an institution and when Stillwater retrieves account data. Plaid and financial institutions have their own privacy practices. See Plaid's End User Privacy Policy. Disconnecting an Item in Stillwater requests removal of that app connection at Plaid; it does not itself erase all records that a bank or Plaid may retain under their own policies.

Retention and deletion

The owner's selected retention period is seven years of transaction history, reviewed quarterly. Stillwater provides a review screen and an explicitly confirmed deletion action for older local transactions. Deletion is not automatic. Account labels, categories, rules, budgets, and goals remain until no longer needed and separately removed.

Disconnecting a bank retains local transaction history. Local history can be deleted separately after disconnection. Original CSV files and encrypted backups are separate copies that the owner must also review and remove when no longer needed. Reimporting, reconnecting, or restoring older backups can restore previously deleted records. Database deletion is not a guarantee of forensic erasure from storage devices.

Public website and support

This website uses no analytics scripts, advertising, account forms, or tracking cookies set by Stillwater. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process IP addresses and routine request information under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If you contact the support email, the email provider processes the message and Stillwater's owner uses it to respond. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, bank credentials, or financial statements. Support correspondence should be reviewed quarterly and deleted within twelve months after resolution unless an ongoing security or service matter requires retention.

Choices, questions, and changes

The owner controls account linking, imports, retrieval, disconnection, and local deletion. For questions, corrections, or requests relating to information received through support, contact stillwater.budget.support@gmail.com.

This notice will be updated when the app's data use materially changes. Expansion to other users, remote hosting, or additional data products requires a privacy and security review before those changes go live.
